The Cancelled Wedding

The Cancelled Wedding

The wedding at Cana, attended by Jesus, where water was turned into wine, was not merely a miracle. It was a prophetic demonstration. The sign at Cana encoded the reversal established in Genesis 48: Ephraim → Manasseh.

That reversal was supposed to appear again in the Book of Revelation. The tribal list in Revelation removes Dan and Ephraim, replacing them with Joseph and Manasseh. The Cana sign was the preview; Revelation was the intended fulfillment.

The prophecy was therefore simple in structure:

  • [Ephraim](ca://s?q=Explain_Ephraim_first_wine) — the first wine, the earlier position, the one displaced.
  • [Manasseh](ca://s?q=Explain_Manasseh_later_wine) — the later wine, the superior position, the one elevated.
  • The Cana sign — the demonstration of the reversal.
  • The Revelation tribal reorder — the intended event completing the reversal.

This page explains why that event — the “wedding” anticipated for nearly two thousand years — never occurred.
